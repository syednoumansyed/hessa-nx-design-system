We use the collected information to:

nx does not share your information with third parties, except as required by law or to provide essential services with trusted partners.

4. Data Security

nx takes data security seriously. We implement industry-standard measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is entirely secure.

5. Your Choices

You may update your account information or request deletion by contacting our support team. However, deleting your information may impact the functionality of your account.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be communicated within the app or via email.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
